🔄 Status Extension Support

Flat-rate preparation support starting at $249.

Temporary status in Canada comes with dates, deadlines, and responsibilities. Whether someone is a visitor, student, or worker, keeping track of expiry dates and extension requirements can become stressful quickly.

Permit & Go offers status extension preparation support for people who want their documents organized before they submit their own extension application. This service is designed for visitors, students, and workers who need help preparing a clear document package.

A status extension file may include passport details, current status documents, proof of activities in Canada, financial documents, education or employment materials, and other supporting records depending on the situation. We help organize these documents and identify common missing categories.

We do not provide legal advice, determine eligibility, complete forms on behalf of clients, submit applications, or communicate with IRCC. Clients remain responsible for all information and submission decisions.

The main value of extension support is timing and organization. Many people wait until close to expiry before gathering documents. This creates unnecessary pressure. A more organized process helps clients understand what they already have and what they still need to collect.

Permit & Go keeps this service intentionally affordable because status extensions are common and often time-sensitive. The goal is to make practical preparation support accessible without making clients feel trapped in large professional fees.

If your status has already expired, if you need restoration, if you have worked or studied without authorization, or if you have any legal complication, contact an authorized immigration professional. For standard preparation and organization before expiry, Permit & Go can help you prepare a cleaner file.
