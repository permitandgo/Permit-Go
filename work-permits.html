💼 Work Permit Support

Flat-rate preparation support starting at $349.

Work permits are one of the most important temporary resident documents for people building a life, career, or next step in Canada. For many applicants, a work permit is connected to employment, financial stability, family planning, and future immigration goals. That is why the preparation stage matters.

Permit & Go offers focused work permit preparation support for clients who want their documents organized clearly before they submit their own application. We help with common work permit situations including open work permits, employer-specific work permits, PGWP-related document support, spousal open work permit document packages, and extension preparation.

Our work permit service is the star service because it is one of the most common needs among international students, recent graduates, temporary workers, and families in Canada. The goal is not to overwhelm clients with legal language or confusing retainers. The goal is to help them understand what documents they have, what may be missing, and how to prepare a cleaner file using publicly available IRCC instructions.

Common issues we help organize include identity documents, status documents, employment-related materials, education-related documents, proof of relationship where relevant, proof of current status in Canada, and other file categories that may apply depending on the applicant’s situation. We do not choose a program for you, determine eligibility, provide immigration strategy, complete forms on your behalf, or submit your application. You remain responsible for your application and all information submitted.

What makes our approach different is simplicity. Many clients do not want a large office experience. They want someone who is responsive, practical, and familiar with how permit files are usually assembled. Permit & Go was created to offer that support at a transparent flat rate.

A well-organized application package cannot guarantee approval, and no honest service should promise that. However, clear preparation can reduce avoidable stress, missing-document confusion, and last-minute scrambling.

If your work permit situation involves a previous refusal, inadmissibility concern, misrepresentation allegation, complicated legal history, or urgent legal question, we recommend contacting an authorized immigration professional. If your situation is a standard document-preparation matter, Permit & Go can help you get organized with clarity and confidence.
