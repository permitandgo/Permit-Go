🎓 Study Permit Support

Flat-rate preparation support starting at $399.

A study permit application is more than a form. It represents a student’s education plan, family investment, financial preparation, and future in Canada. For international students, the process can feel overwhelming because every document seems important and small mistakes can create anxiety.

Permit & Go supports students by helping them organize their study permit materials before they submit their own applications. Our study permit support focuses on document readiness, checklist organization, formatting, and general non-legal information based on public instructions.

Common study permit preparation areas include passport and identity documents, letter of acceptance, proof of financial support, education history, status documents, family information, and any additional materials a student may want to organize before submission. We can help identify missing document categories and create a clearer structure for the file.

Many students also feel confused when they need to extend their permit, change schools, or prepare for a new program. In those situations, having documents arranged clearly can make the process feel less chaotic. Permit & Go does not decide whether a student qualifies, does not write legal arguments, does not choose a program, and does not communicate with IRCC. Students remain responsible for their own forms, answers, declarations, and submission.

The value of our service is preparation. We help clients slow down, organize the file, and understand the difference between a rushed application and a carefully assembled one. We also keep pricing transparent so students and families know the cost before starting.

Study permits are especially sensitive because families often invest significant money into tuition, travel, and settlement costs. That is why credibility matters. Permit & Go is built on industry experience and a permit-focused process, not vague promises.

No one can guarantee a study permit approval. If a case involves a previous refusal, complex legal issue, inadmissibility concern, or need for legal strategy, the right next step is to contact an authorized immigration professional. For students who need organized document support and a clearer preparation process, Permit & Go offers a straightforward and accessible option.
