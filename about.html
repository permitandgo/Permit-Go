About

Built for people who want clear permit support without big-firm confusion.

Permit & Go was created after years of industry exposure working alongside experienced Canadian immigration professionals. The idea is simple: many clients need organized, affordable, and responsive preparation support before they submit their own permit applications.

Large firms can be expensive and intimidating. Informal help can be unreliable. Permit & Go sits in the middle by offering a more structured, transparent, and personal experience for common permit categories.

Our focus is permits: study permits, work permits, visitor visas, status extensions, and family-related temporary resident permit files. We keep the process clear, the pricing simple, and the communication direct.

Our boundaries

Credibility means being honest about what we can and cannot do. Permit & Go is not an RCIC, RISIA, law firm, or authorized representative. We do not provide legal advice, immigration advice, eligibility decisions, program selection, form completion on behalf of clients, representation, or communication with IRCC.

For legal questions, refusals, inadmissibility concerns, appeals, or complex immigration histories, clients should contact an authorized professional.
