FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are you an RCIC or immigration lawyer?

No. Permit & Go is not an RCIC, RISIA, law firm, or authorized immigration representative.

What can Permit & Go help with?

We can help organize documents, prepare checklists, identify missing categories, format files, and provide general non-legal information based on publicly available instructions.

What can Permit & Go not do?

We cannot provide immigration advice, legal advice, eligibility assessments, program selection, legal strategy, form completion on your behalf, submission on your behalf, representation, or IRCC communication.

Who should use an authorized representative?

Anyone with a refusal, inadmissibility issue, misrepresentation concern, status violation, appeal, complicated family history, or legal question should contact an authorized professional.

Can anyone guarantee approval?

No. No honest service can guarantee approval. Final decisions are made by the Government of Canada.

Do I submit my own application?

Yes. You remain responsible for your application, forms, answers, documents, declarations, and submission.
